Privacy Policy

What is collected

When you create a member account, the following information is collected: email address, name, country, and affiliation/origin (all required), and phone number (optional).

Why it is collected

Membership on this site is approval-based. This information is used solely to verify who you are, decide account approval, and manage download access. It is never sold, shared, or used for marketing.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a private Supabase project (hosted in the Seoul region) managed directly and exclusively by the site owner, Minseok Choi. When you sign up, a one-time notification containing your signup details is also emailed to the site owner.

Community content

Posts and images shared in the Community section are visible to approved members only. You can delete your own posts at any time.

Your rights

You may request to view, correct, or delete your data at any time by emailing minseok0640@gmail.com. Deletion requests are honored promptly, and deleting your account removes your profile data.
